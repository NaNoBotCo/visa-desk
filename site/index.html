Questions

Which border crossing do you use?

It depends on your visa and which northern crossings are running the week you travel. The crossing is named in your quote before you book.

How long does a border run take?

Most runs from Chiang Mai are a single long day, door to door. Crossings further out are quoted as an overnight.

Do you publish the immigration rules?

No. Requirements move, and a page of rules ages badly. We check the current requirement for your visa type and your travel date, and the quote reflects it.

Who is this for?

Long-stay residents of northern Thailand — DTV holders, retirees, people on marriage, education and business visas, and anyone who needs to leave and re-enter periodically.

What does it cost?

Each job is quoted in writing for your visa type, your dates and your pickup point. Pricing sits with the other Chiang Mai agencies.

Do I have to go to the office myself?

Some filings need you in person and some do not. The quote says which, and someone goes with you when you do.

Can you take a group?

Yes. Couples, housemates and coworking floors go on one booking and one price.
