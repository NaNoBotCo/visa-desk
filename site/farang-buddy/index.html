FarangBuddy

A stay tracker for Thailand. Tell it when you entered, left, ran the border or extended, and it keeps the date that matters in front of you.

Opens in a browser. Its records sit in the browser on your device.

What it keeps track of

One list of what happened. Everything else is worked out from it.

Your admitted-until date

The one from the stamp, not from a guess. The app shows how to read it if you are not sure which of the two dates it is.

Entries, exits and border runs

A same-day run in and out is one entry in the log. So is an extension done inside the country.

90-day reporting and TM30

Both dates sit on the status page with everything else, so neither is the one you forget.

More than one person

A partner, a parent, a household. Each person has their own log and their own dates.

Currency

A converter, for when the paperwork asks for a number in baht and your bank thinks in something else.

Your data, exportable

Records live in the browser on your device, and the app will hand them back to you as a file.

Put it on your home screen

It is a web app, so there is no store and nothing to update. Once it is on the home screen it opens like any other app.

Point a phone camera at this. Then, in the browser that opens:

  1. iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: the three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Desktop: the install icon in the address bar.

Or open farangbuddy.netlify.app directly.

It is a reminder, not proof

The app works dates out from what you type into it. The stamp in your passport and the officer in front of you are right and the app is not. Use it to see a date coming; use the desk to do something about it.
