What you get

A working site with your name on it, not a template you have to finish.

The whole site

Services, how it works, the questions, the enquiry form and the free app page. Complete in both English and Thai.

Your name on it

Your business name, your city, your domain, your email, your LINE Official Account and your share image. One command changes all of them.

Your partner codes

A tool that makes partner codes. Type a partner's name and you get a code, a link, a QR image and a card you can print for their counter. Their bookings are counted to them.

Enquiries that arrive

We host the system that receives your enquiries. It sends them to your own list of people and keeps a record of every one.

Statements

A file for each month with the totals by service and by partner code. You can send it with an invoice.

Updates

New pages, new languages and fixes built here also go into your copy. Any words you changed stay as you wrote them.

How it goes

Tell us the province

Your business, your city, the services you really offer, and whether you already have a domain and a LINE Official Account.

We brand and deploy it

We put your name and your city on it, and your colours if you want them. It runs on your domain, and email arrives where you read it.

You get the keys

A licence number, the partner code tool, the monthly totals tool, and someone to ask when you need a change.

What it costs

There is a set-up fee to build it and put your name on it. After that you pay a licence, monthly or yearly, and hosting is included. The rate is lower if you run your own system, and higher if we receive your enquiries and produce your monthly totals.

The price depends on your province and on how much you want us to run for you. Ask, and you get a written price.

Every licensed site keeps one line at the bottom saying who built it. That line is part of the licence. Everything else on the site is yours to change.

Questions

Can I change the words?

Yes, all of them, in both languages: the services, the questions and the prices you give. You are licensing the structure, not the sentences.

Do you take a share of my bookings?

Only on the option where we receive your enquiries and produce your totals. On the plain licence you keep everything and pay the licence fee.

Can I run it on my own hosting?

Yes. It is plain files and one small program, so it runs almost anywhere. That is the lower rate.

Do I need a LINE Official Account?

You should have one. It is free to create, and LINE is where people in Thailand reply. Without it, the LINE buttons on the site do not appear.

What about the US visa pages?

Those pages are a separate business and are not in the standard licence. Say so in the form if you want them.

Who owns the domain?

You do. Buy it in your own name and we connect it. If the licence ends, the domain and everything you wrote stay with you.
