Chiang Mai Visa Desk prepares and files the paperwork that keeps long-stay residents of northern Thailand in status.

· Work permits — the Non-B, the permit, the tax ID and social security registration prepared as one file. New permits, renewals and a change of employer.
· Thai company registration — registration, shareholder and director paperwork, tax ID and VAT, the corporate bank account, and the work permit the company then issues.
· Retirement — Non-O and Non-OA, the annual extension, bank letters and account paperwork, insurance documents, and the 90-day reports that follow.
